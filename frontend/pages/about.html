How It All Started

The Story Behind
Tokimi Foundation

Tokimi Foundation was born from a simple but powerful observation — that thousands of senior secondary students in Nigeria were graduating without basic computer skills, putting them at a severe disadvantage in an increasingly digital world.

Our founders recognized that access to technology education should not be a privilege reserved for those who can afford private schools or expensive computer centers. Every student deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and compete in the digital economy.

What started as a small weekend program for a handful of students has grown into a structured, impactful organization that has trained hundreds of students across multiple batches — and we are just getting started.
