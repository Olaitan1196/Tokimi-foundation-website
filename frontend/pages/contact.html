Quick answers to the questions we get asked the most

Visit our Enrollment page and fill in the student's details. You can also walk into our office during working hours and our staff will assist you with the process.

Tokimi Foundation is a not-for-profit organization and our programs are offered at minimal or no cost to qualifying students. Contact us to learn about fees and scholarship opportunities.

Our standard computer literacy program runs for 12 weeks, covering all five core courses. Students who complete the full program receive a certificate of completion.

Scholarships and grants are available to enrolled students who demonstrate academic excellence or financial need. Applications are reviewed by our scholarship committee on a case-by-case basis.
