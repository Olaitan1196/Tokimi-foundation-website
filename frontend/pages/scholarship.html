🎓 How Our Scholarship Works

The Tokimi Foundation scholarship supports students in Nigerian public secondary schools, with a focus on Osun State. Beneficiaries are chosen based on a combination of academic performance and financial need. Shortlisted students sit for a qualifying examination, and those who pass are awarded the scholarship.

Who Can Qualify?

  • Currently enrolled in a public secondary school, primarily within Osun State
  • Demonstrates strong academic performance
  • Comes from a financially disadvantaged background
  • Successfully passes the foundation's qualifying examination
